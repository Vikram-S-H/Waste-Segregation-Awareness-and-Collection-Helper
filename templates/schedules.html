Collection Days and Timings

Check the sample collection schedule below and prepare your waste accordingly.

Note: These are sample demonstration schedules for this project, not verified municipal collection timings.

🌱 Wet Waste

Collection Day: Monday, Wednesday and Friday

Sample Time: 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM

Keep suitable kitchen and garden waste separate.

♻️ Dry Waste

Collection Day: Tuesday and Saturday

Sample Time: 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Keep recyclable materials clean and dry.

⚠️ Hazardous Waste

Collection Day: Thursday

Sample Time: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Use authorised collection facilities for batteries, chemicals and other hazardous waste.

Collection Instructions

  • Separate wet and dry waste before collection.
  • Use suitable and covered waste containers.
  • Keep recyclable materials clean and dry.
  • Never mix hazardous waste with regular household waste.
  • Place waste out only according to the applicable collection instructions.
  • Follow your local municipal guidelines.
